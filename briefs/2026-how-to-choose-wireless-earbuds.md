## Review themes (paraphrased)

### Praise
1. Seal/tips decide ANC and bass more than brand ads.
2. Ecosystem pairing (Apple/Samsung) beats cross-platform for many owners.
3. Bose/Sony/Apple all can deliver “dangerous” quiet when fit is right.
4. Soundcore wins call mic NC + multipoint value.
5. Fit upgrades (Pro 3 foam tips, wings) fix workout slip.

### Complaints
1. Premium price / incremental upgrades from last gen.
2. Touch/pinch misfires; hair catches stems.
3. Case battery quirks (AirPods case reserve down; Galaxy idle drain; Soundcore plastic case).
4. Translation/AI features uneven.
5. Longevity: rare single-bud failure after ~17 months (Sony owner).

## Your call
