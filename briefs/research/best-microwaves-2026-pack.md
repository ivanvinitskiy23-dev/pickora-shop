## Owner themes (paraphrase only)
Praise clusters:
- Inverter models genuinely eliminate the "frozen center / rubbery edges" issue when thawing chicken or ground beef.
- The mute beep feature on Toshiba and Comfee' is universally praised by early risers and parents of sleeping children.
- Breville's soft-close door eliminates the typical loud metal slam, making the appliance feel significantly higher end.
- Panasonic HomeChef saves considerable counter real estate by replacing a separate toaster oven and air fryer.

Complaint clusters:
- 700W compact models require ~30–40% longer heating times for frozen dinners compared to 1100W+ full-size models.
- Dark tinted windows on several modern models make it hard to watch butter or sauces bubbling without opening the door.
- Combo air fryer units (like HomeChef) require interior cleaning immediately after air frying to avoid baked-on grease during subsequent microwave sessions.

## Get if / Skip if
- **Toshiba EM131A5C-BS**: Get if you want an affordable, dependable daily microwave with accurate sensors and silent beeps. Skip if you need true inverter continuous simmering.
- **Panasonic NN-SN686S**: Get if you frequently defrost raw meats, soften butter, or reheat delicate dishes evenly. Skip if you prefer push-button dial minimalism.
- **Breville The Smooth Wave**: Get if door-slam clatter drives you crazy and you want luxury kitchen finishes with precise sensor intelligence. Skip if spending over $300 on a microwave makes no sense for your budget.
- **Comfee' EM720CPL**: Get if your counter space is minimal or you only reheat coffee and leftovers. Skip if you cook large casserole dishes or expect rapid 60-second hot tea.
- **Panasonic HomeChef 4-in-1**: Get if you want to replace multiple bulky appliances with one versatile stainless unit. Skip if you already own a dedicated high-capacity air fryer.
