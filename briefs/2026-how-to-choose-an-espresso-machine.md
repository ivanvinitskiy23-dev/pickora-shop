## Review themes (paraphrased)

### Praise
1. Bambino Plus: daily reliability for 1–2 years, café savings, easy auto milk, tiny NYC footprint.
2. Bambino: durable years of daily shots, fast heat, beginner-friendly pressurized start, room to grow with 54mm parts.
3. Gaggia: metal build, repairability, café-quality once dialed, strong community/mods.
4. Dedica Duo: looks, speed, compact, better than cheaper De’Longhi units.
5. Stilosa: cheap entry that can taste shop-good with fresh beans + technique; fast heat.
6. Impress: assisted tamp + built-in grinder consistency; hot water for Americanos; lasting daily use.

### Complaints
1. Bambino Plus: auto-steam mid-stop quirk; plastic in portafilter; light machine slides when locking; drip tray fills; cleaning manual outdated.
2. Bambino: plastic feel / obsolescence worry; hard water; weak vs milk-first machines if technique off.
3. Gaggia: slow steam transition; learning curve; needs good grinder and bottled/soft water.
4. Dedica: early jamming until learned; steam light quirks; pitcher not included.
5. Stilosa: not auto shot stop; pressurized stock = fake crema unless modified; plastic tamper junk; tall mugs won’t fit.
6. Impress: learning curve; hopper beans stick; no low-water sensor (dry pump risk); froth skill needed; expensive cleaning supplies.

## Your call

- Criteria-first guide: grinder > machine; pressurized vs single-wall; auto vs manual steam; 51/54/58mm; footprint; maintenance.
- **Best easy milk drinks:** Bambino Plus

- **Best slim mid:** Dedica Duo
- **Best budget trial:** Stilosa (with caveats)
- **Best one-box with grinder:** Barista Express Impress
