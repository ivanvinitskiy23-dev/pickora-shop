## Review themes (your words)

### Praise clusters
1. SCA / correct brew temp and bloom make drip taste closer to pour-over when beans and grind are right (OXO, Moccamaster).
2. Programmable wake-up brew and clear controls matter more than extra “modes” for daily households (OXO, Ninja, Cuisinart).
3. Thermal carafes avoid hot-plate “cooked” coffee; glass + plate stays hotter for some who drink fast (split preference).
4. Removable / easy-clean reservoirs and honest “Rich” or Bold strength settings (Ninja models, Cuisinart).
5. Capsule convenience with stronger crema than typical K-Cups (Nespresso Vertuo); DualBrew covers mixed households (pods + grounds).

### Complaint clusters
1. Basket drip valves / springs stick open → leaks and burns when carafe is removed mid-brew (OXO cluster; some Cuisinart basket seating issues).
2. Thermal carafe seam water / mold fear; lids that trap moisture (OXO long-term updates).
3. Plastic feel, knobs, thin glass carafes, and post-warranty leaks / clock failures (Ninja durability cluster).
4. Overflow / grounds in reservoir when overfilled or basket not seated (Cuisinart near “14 cup” max; fine grind on Moccamaster).
5. Moccamaster: high price, no timer, pour spout mess, many hand-clean parts; Nespresso: capsule cost and locked ecosystem.

## Your call

- **Best overall drip (programmable + taste):** OXO Brew 9-Cup (thermal) — with clear valve/carafe caveats
