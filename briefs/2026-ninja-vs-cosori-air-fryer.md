## Review themes (paraphrased)

### Praise clusters
1. Dual Ninja baskets + Smart Finish = real weeknight win for teens/families cooking two foods.
2. Probe on DZ550 removes guesswork on steak/chicken doneness.
3. Cosori TurboBlaze cooks lean chicken juicy/fast; quieter than many prior units; easy wipe cleanup.
4. Both beat oven heat-up and keep kitchens cooler; frozen snacks and weeknight proteins shine.
5. Cosori looks premium; ceramic basket era praised when confirmed by late manufacture date.

### Complaint clusters
1. Ninja dual baskets get soggy if overfilled — shake/space still matter; grease builds fast without liners.
2. Ninja is large / counter-heavy; bacon drips dry for some; non-breaded thin meats can dry out.
3. Cosori can miss “done” chime; temp resets after cycle; top panel awkward for shorter users.
4. Cosori accessory ecosystem thinner than Ninja; silicone liner sizing fiddly.
5. Ceramic vs older PTFE confusion on Cosori packaging; fire reports often tied to misuse (e.g. on a stove).
