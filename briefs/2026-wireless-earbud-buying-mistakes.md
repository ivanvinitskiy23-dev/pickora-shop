## Themes (paraphrased)

**AirPods:** great for iPhone; fit still tip-critical; some need hooks; ANC strong on planes when sealed; Conversation Awareness flaky; battery often ~5h vs 8h claim; EQ limited.

**Soundcore:** excellent mics/calls; multipoint; case screen mixed/gimmick; ANC behind Bose; sound/EQ subjective (single-driver critiques); sweat can glitch BT; price debate vs cheaper Soundcore.

**Bose:** class-leading hush when sealed; wings help fit; huge case; touch controls annoying; app/internet quirks on Android; seal decides whether ANC “works at all.”
