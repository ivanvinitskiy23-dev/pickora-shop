## Review themes (paraphrased)

### Praise
1. Combo Toshiba units save space and replace multiple gadgets when they work.
2. Panasonic commercial feels durable, cleans easily without a turntable, lasts years.
3. Sharp dial control and even heating delight people who hate presets.
4. Chefman looks sleek and fits small counters for light reheats.
5. Child-lock-in-handle designs beat two-step locks for some owners.

### Complaints
1. Toshiba combo smoke/spark/overheat reports and support frustration.
2. Short consumer microwave lifespan vs commercial.
3. Loud commercial beeps (usually mutable).
4. Chefman stiff keypad / rare no-heat failures after return window.
5. Sharp dial time limit and some early magnetron failures.

## Your call

- Longevity: Panasonic NE-1054F
- Simple dial: Sharp R-21LCFS
- Combo only with attended use: Toshiba models
- Budget light use: Chefman
