## Review themes (your words)

### Praise clusters
1. **Inverter precision:** Panasonic and Breville continuous power genuinely stops cold centers and overcooked rubbery outer rings when defrosting ground beef or chicken breasts.
2. **Buzzer mute option:** Toshiba, Comfee', and Breville allow toggling the end-of-cycle chime completely silent—a major relief in small apartments, early mornings, and open-plan homes.
3. **Soft-close mechanics:** The Breville eliminates the harsh metallic door slam typical of cheap microwave latches.
4. **Appliance consolidation:** The Panasonic HomeChef replaces both a standalone countertop toaster oven and an air fryer for small kitchens needing counter space back.
5. **Humidity sensor accuracy:** Toshiba's sensor cooking reliably prevents overcooking soup, fresh veggies, and standard dinner plates.

### Complaint clusters
1. **Low-wattage speed tax:** The 700W Comfee' takes noticeably longer (~30–40% extra time) to bring refrigerated meals or soup to steaming temperatures.
2. **Door tint opacity:** Several modern dark-finish doors make it difficult to monitor food splatter or bubbling without cracking open the door.
3. **Combo interior cleaning:** The HomeChef’s stainless interior requires wiping immediately after roasting or air frying to prevent grease from baking onto walls during microwave runs.
4. **Countertop depth clearance:** 1.2 cu. ft. and combo models require at least 3 inches of rear/side clearance for heat dissipation, challenging shallow 20-inch counters.
