## Review themes (paraphrased — test brief)

### Praise clusters
1. Stainless everyday kettles heat a full mug set quickly and wipe clean.
2. Auto shut-off and boil-dry protection calm apartment renters.
3. Gooseneck owners praise slow, even pours for V60 / Chemex.
4. Variable temp on gooseneck models helps green tea and light roasts.
5. Both beat waiting for a cloudy microwave mug.

### Complaint clusters
1. Wide-spout kettles splash and overshoot pour-over filters.
2. Mineral scale builds fast on hard water — descaling needed.
3. Gooseneck capacity feels small for guests / family tea.
4. Some plastic lids or handles feel cheap on budget stainless units.
5. Base cords and counter footprint annoy tiny kitchens.

| Product | Praise | Complaints |
|---|---|---|
| Cosori stainless | Speed, capacity, easy wipe | Coarse pour, scale, generic look |
| Gooseneck | Pour control, temp hold, looks premium | Small tank, price, slower household boiling |
