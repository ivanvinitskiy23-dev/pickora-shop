Who should skip this

  • Skip a dual-basket Ninja if your counter is already packed; the extra basket is useless if you cannot leave the machine out.
  • Skip the Breville if you only reheat fries a few nights a week, because it is a full countertop oven rather than a small gadget.

Detailed Reviews

1. Ninja Foodi DZ550 — Best Overall Dual Basket Air Fryer

The Ninja Foodi DZ550 remains one of the strongest performers in 2026, especially for families and anyone who wants to cook two different dishes at once.

Key Specs

  • Capacity: 10 QT (2 × 5 QT baskets)
  • Power: 1690W
  • Functions: Air fry, roast, bake, reheat, dehydrate, broil
  • Smart Features: Smart Finish, Match Cook, temperature probe

Pros

  • Excellent dual-zone cooking with independent controls
  • Smart Finish syncs both baskets perfectly
  • Large capacity for family meals or meal prep
  • Crispy results with minimal oil
  • Easy-to-use digital interface

Cons

  • Takes up significant counter space
  • Higher price point
  • Slight learning curve if you’re new to air fryers

Performance The dual-basket spec is why the DZ550 ranks for cooking two foods at once. Owner notes on large batches are stronger than for most single-basket models.

Verdict Get this if you cook for three or more and want two foods done at once. Skip it if your counter is already full.

Buy it if: You value convenience and cook multiple items regularly. Skip it if: You have very limited counter space.

Buying Guide — How to Choose the Best Air Fryer in 2026

  • Capacity: 4 QT (1-2 people) → 6 QT (small family) → 8-10+ QT (large family/meal prep)
  • Single vs Dual Basket: Dual saves huge time
  • Power: 1700W+ is ideal
  • Cleaning: Look for dishwasher-safe parts
  • Extras: Sync functions, temperature probe, glass viewing

FAQ — Best Air Fryers 2026

1. Are air fryers worth it in 2026?

Yes for weeknight fries and leftovers. Skip one if you already have a countertop oven you use every day.

2. What is the best air fryer overall in this guide?

The Ninja Foodi DZ550 is the top pick for families who can spare the counter space.

3. Do air fryers use a lot of electricity?

They typically draw less energy than heating a full oven for small batches.

4. Can I cook frozen food in an air fryer?

Yes. Frozen fries and wings are the most common use in owner reviews.

5. Which air fryer is best for families?

Dual-basket models such as the Ninja DZ550 or Philips, if the footprint fits.

6. Are Cosori air fryers reliable?

Owner reviews are generally strong for daily 6-quart use. It is not a dual-basket family machine.

7. Do air fryers need preheating?

Some do. Newer models preheat quickly; check the manual for the exact model.

8. Are glass air fryers safe?

The Ninja Crispi uses tempered heat-resistant glass. Capacity is smaller than 8-quart baskets.

Conclusion

If you cook for a household, get the Ninja Foodi DZ550. If you want something simpler and faster, get the Cosori TurboBlaze. The Chefman is the cheap family option. The Breville only makes sense if you were already shopping for a countertop oven.
