## How to use

1. Pick a **P1** row with status `idea`.
2. Copy `briefs/_TEMPLATE.md` → `briefs/YYYY-slug.md` and fill it (20–40 min Amazon research) including **Media** and **Chips**.
3. Set status to `brief` and put the brief path in the last column.
4. Open a chat as the **Article Agent** (`AGENTS.md`): ask for draft / cover / publish.
5. Commands: `черновик из brief …` → `обложку` or drop photo in `briefs/media-inbox/` → `выпусти статью` → `закоммить и запушь`.
6. After live: status `live`. Chips on the articles hub card must match the Chips column.
